# Glossary

The Spanish edition of this book, the original Excel workbook and the engine's identifiers use
Spanish gear terminology. This table gives the English term used in this edition next to each
Spanish one, with a short definition. It is sorted by the English term.

| Spanish | English | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Ángulo de ataque | Angle of attack | Angle between a surface (cut edge, otter board, bridle, sweep) and the direction of the flow. |
| Lastre | Ballast | Weight (chain, leads) added to the footrope to keep it in contact with the seabed. |
| Pata, barra (B) | Bar | One of the four sides of a mesh, of length half the stretched mesh size; in cut codes, a cut that advances half a mesh in depth and reduces the width by half a mesh. |
| Lote | Batch | A set of nets or design cases computed together from a spreadsheet. |
| Vientre | Belly | The lower panel of the net, opposite the top panel. |
| Lista de materiales | Bill of materials | List of netting, ropes, floats, chain and fittings needed to build the gear, with quantities. |
| Cuerpo | Body | The tapered part of the net between the square (or wings) and the extension or codend. |
| Tiro a punto fijo | Bollard pull | Pull the vessel can exert at zero speed, tied to a fixed point. |
| Bocana | Bosom | The central part of the net mouth along the footrope, between the wings. |
| Red de arrastre de fondo, red de fondo | Bottom trawl | Trawl towed in contact with the seabed. |
| Potencia al freno | Brake power (BHP) | Power delivered by the engine at the shaft, before propeller losses. |
| Bridas | Bridles | Wires or ropes that connect the wing ends to the sweeps. |
| Flotación | Buoyancy | Upward force provided by floats on the headrope; it keeps the net mouth open vertically. |
| Calibración | Calibration | Adjustment of model coefficients so that results match measurements. |
| Copo, bolsa | Codend | The rear end of the net, where the catch accumulates. |
| Hélice de paso variable (PV) | Controllable pitch propeller (PV) | Propeller whose blade pitch can be changed in operation. |
| Pies de gallo | Crowfoot | Short legs that split a line into two or more attachment points. |
| Corte | Cut | Way in which a netting panel is cut on the diagonal to change its width, given as a code (e.g. 1N2B). |
| Razón de corte | Cutting rate | Ratio between the meshes lost in width and the meshes advanced in depth by a cut. |
| Dan leno | Dan leno | Spreader bar or bobbin at the wing end that keeps the wing open and protects it on the seabed. |
| Resistencia | Drag (resistance) | Hydrodynamic force opposing the motion of the net, the otter boards, the floats or the warps. |
| Túnel | Extension | Untapered section of netting between the body and the codend. |
| Índice de filtración | Filtration index | Ratio $d/l$ of twine diameter to mesh size, summed over the net. |
| Marea | Fishing trip | A voyage of the vessel from port to the fishing ground and back. |
| Hélice de paso fijo (PF) | Fixed pitch propeller (PF) | Propeller whose blade pitch cannot be changed. |
| Flota | Fleet | A group of vessels, in this book used for projecting nets onto many vessels at once. |
| Boya | Float | Buoyant sphere attached to the headrope. |
| Burlón, relinga inferior | Footrope | Lower rope of the net mouth, in contact with the seabed. |
| Ensuciamiento | Fouling | Growth or accumulation on the hull, propeller or netting that increases drag. |
| Embande, coeficiente de armado | Hanging ratio | Ratio $U_1$ between the mounted length of netting and its stretched length. |
| Lance | Haul (tow) | One trawling operation, from shooting to hauling the net. |
| Lima, relinga superior | Headrope | Upper rope of the net mouth, which carries the floats. |
| Abertura horizontal | Horizontal opening | Width of the net mouth while towed (distance between wingtips). |
| Toma, pegadura | Joining | Sewing of two panels of different mesh counts together, taking up meshes at a fixed rhythm. |
| Nudo | Knot | Junction of the twine at the corners of each mesh. |
| Nudos | Knots | Unit of speed, one nautical mile per hour (0.5144 m/s). |
| Malla | Mesh | The diamond-shaped opening of the netting, formed by four bars and four knots. |
| Tamaño de malla, mallero | Mesh size | Stretched length of a mesh, between the centres of two opposite knots. |
| Taller de redes | Net loft | Workshop where nets are built and repaired. |
| Redero | Net maker | Person who builds and repairs nets. |
| Paño | Netting panel | A trapezoidal piece of netting; the net is made of panels sewn together. |
| Tobera | Nozzle | Ring around the propeller that increases thrust at low speed. |
| Portón | Otter board (door) | Hydrodynamic board that spreads the net horizontally. |
| Armador | Owner | Owner or operator of a fishing vessel. |
| Filado | Paid out | Length of warp let out from the winch. |
| Punto, nudo (N) | Point | In cut codes, a cut that advances one mesh in depth without changing the width. |
| Tiro | Pull | Towing force the vessel can deliver at a given speed. |
| Armado | Rigging (mounting) | Mounting of netting on ropes and the set of components that connect the net to the vessel. |
| Rendimiento | Runnage | Length of twine per unit mass (m/kg), the inverse of Rtex. |
| Fondo (marino) | Seabed | The bottom of the sea over which the trawl is towed. |
| Selectividad | Selectivity | The ability of the net, especially the codend mesh, to release fish below a given size. |
| Patrón | Skipper | Master of a fishing vessel. |
| Superficie sólida | Solid (twine) area | Area projected by the twine of the net, length of twine times diameter. |
| Solidez | Solidity | Fraction of the working area blocked by twine, $S_s/S_t$. |
| Cielo, visera, cuadro | Square | Panel of the top of the net that overhangs the footrope, so that fish do not escape upwards. |
| Malla estirada | Stretched mesh | Mesh pulled taut along its length, the reference for measuring mesh size. |
| Patentes | Sweeps | Wires that connect the otter boards to the bridles. |
| Dorso | Top panel | The upper panel of the net. |
| Velocidad de arrastre | Towing speed | Speed of the vessel through the water while trawling. |
| Malla transversal (T) | Transversal (mesh) | In cut codes, a cut that reduces the width by one mesh without advancing. |
| Red de arrastre | Trawl (net) | Funnel-shaped net towed by a vessel. |
| Arrastrero | Trawler | Vessel that fishes with trawls. |
| Hilo | Twine | The yarn or braided thread from which netting is made. |
| Título del hilo | Twine size (Rtex) | Linear density of the twine, in grams per kilometre. |
| Incertidumbre | Uncertainty | Range of plausible values of a result given the uncertainty of its inputs and coefficients. |
| Aula UTN | UTN Classroom | The app's teaching mode: exercises with marking, guided labs and courses. |
| Abertura vertical | Vertical opening | Height of the net mouth while towed. |
| Cable de remolque, cable de cala | Warp | Wire connecting the vessel to the otter boards. |
| Ala | Wing | Side extension of the net mouth that herds fish towards the body. |
| Punta de ala | Wingtip | The forward end of a wing. |
| Superficie de trabajo | Working area | Area of the mounted netting, depending on the hanging ratio. |
